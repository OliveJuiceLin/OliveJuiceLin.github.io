## 📖 About Me

I am an undergraduate student at Xi'an Jiaotong University, majoring in Computer Science and Technology, supervised by Prof. Minnan Luo.

My research interests are generally open. Now, I focus on **large language model interpretability**, **Knowledge Conflicts in LLMs**, and **Improving LLM reasoning using RL**.
